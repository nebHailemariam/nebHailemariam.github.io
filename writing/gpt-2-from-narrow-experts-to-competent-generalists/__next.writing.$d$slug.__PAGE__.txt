6:["$","p","p-8",{"children":"The paper utilizes Byte Pair Encoding (BPE), which provides a middle ground between character-level and word-level tokenization. Character-level tokenizers represent text using individual characters, resulting in substantially longer token sequences and consequently greater computational costs for sequence models. In contrast, word-level tokenizers can suffer from large vocabularies and poor handling of rare or unseen words, particularly in large-scale datasets such as the One Billion Word Benchmark [10]. BPE addresses these limitations by representing text using frequently occurring subword units, which allows it to reduce vocabulary size and efficiently represent unseen words."}]
